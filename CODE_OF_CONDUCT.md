# Code of conduct

Parcel Post contributors are expected to make participation welcoming and
harassment-free regardless of experience, identity, background, or personal
characteristics.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and keep technical
disagreement focused on the work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language,
doxxing, and publication of another person's private delivery or account data
are not acceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's GitHub profile
or the repository's private security-reporting channel. Maintainers may edit or
remove contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when
needed to protect the community.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and applies in repository
spaces as well as related public project discussions.
