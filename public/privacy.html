Delivery Tracker · French and Swiss parcels

Privacy notice

Effective 30 August 2026

Delivery Tracker stores only the information needed to authenticate you and follow French and Swiss parcel deliveries. It does not sell personal data, serve ads, or include third-party behavioral analytics.

What is processed

  • Your email address, account ID, session metadata, authentication events, and basic profile data when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Parcel labels, tracking numbers, carriers, status, tracking history, a delivery postcode supplied for a DPD Switzerland or Mondial Relay parcel, and a Colis Privé combined tracking credential that can contain the parcel's delivery postcode.
  • Optional Planzer shared and Dachser Customer Iberia capability links.
  • Push endpoints, encryption keys, browser user agent, and delivery acknowledgements when you enable notifications.
  • Network metadata processed by the hosting, proxy, authentication, and email infrastructure, such as IP address, timestamp, and user agent.

How it is used

Supabase provides authentication and database access. Google provides social sign-in, and the configured SMTP provider sends codes when email OTP is enabled. Cloudflare and the container host may process network metadata. The selected carrier receives its tracking number or carrier-specific tracking credential so Delivery Tracker can retrieve updates. DPD Switzerland may also receive the parcel's supplied postcode for recipient verification. Mondial Relay requires the five-digit recipient postcode to retrieve shipment events. Colis Privé receives a combined credential made from its 12-character shipment number and the five-digit delivery postcode. Planzer receives a shared-link capability when you provide one. Dachser receives its supplied capability link; sender, recipient, address, contact, and document fields in Dachser's response are discarded. Web Push endpoints receive encrypted notifications you requested.

Retention and your controls

Parcel data remains until you delete the account. Archiving hides a parcel from the active list but retains its history. Infrastructure backups and security logs may remain for their operator's limited retention period.

Use Download my data in the account menu for a JSON export. Use Delete account to permanently remove your Auth user, parcels, events, and push subscriptions. Signing out clears account-scoped local state, including the offline parcel snapshot; browser controls can clear the remaining PWA cache and notification permission.

Security and contact

The service uses HTTPS, short-lived access tokens, rotating refresh tokens, Postgres row-level security, account rate limits, and server-only secrets. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Report a privacy or security concern through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting. Never put a real tracking number or combined tracking credential, delivery postcode, sign-in code, token, Planzer shared link, or Dachser detail link in a public issue.

This notice covers the official Delivery Tracker service for French and Swiss parcel tracking. Third-party forks are operated independently and must publish their own notice.
